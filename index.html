You were invited

Four steps to the session

All you need is Golether on your computer and the link your friend sent you.

  1. Click the link

    Your browser asks whether to open Golether. Say yes.

  2. Confirm

    Golether shows the name of the session and who invites you. Press “Join”.

  3. Compare the code

    You and the host see the same three words and a number. Say them to each other aloud.

    mapleriverstone42

  4. Enjoy

    The host lets you in, and the film or the photos appear for everybody at the same moment.

The link did not open the app? Ask the host for the key instead: copy it, open Golether, paste it into the “Invitation” field and press “Connect”. A link works for 15 minutes, a key for up to an hour; the whole company can join with one invitation.

You invite

Show a film or photos

The film and the photos stay on your computer and go straight to your guests.
Up to five people watch together.

  1. Choose what to show

    Press “Create a session” and choose a film, a photo or a whole folder. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

  2. Send the invitation

    Press “Invite”, copy the link and post it in your group chat. Everyone joins with the same link.

  3. Let them in

    Compare the code with your guest by voice, admit them and press play.

Show a film or a whole folder of photos. Leaf through the album together, or open a photo just for yourself and zoom into a detail.

Drawing over the picture

Circle a detail right on the frame of a film or on a photo: everyone sees the line, and each person draws in their own colour.

Reactions and chat

Emoji float over the picture with the sender's name, and the chat is right beside it.

🙂

Faces and voices

Everyone's webcam and voice next to the picture, with echo cancellation and a volume for each person.

💬

Your own subtitles and audio

Everyone picks subtitles and an audio track on their own, independently of the others.

Original quality

The film is not re-encoded into a blurry stream: everyone watches the file as it is.
